01 / ABOUT ME

From security fundamentals
to SOC investigations.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning with a strong interest in cybersecurity and Security Operations.

My cybersecurity learning began with computer fundamentals, networking, security basics, Windows and Linux security, SOC fundamentals, vulnerability management and blue-team concepts.

I gradually moved into practical security exercises, security projects and ethical hacking. This helped me understand how attackers discover weaknesses, perform reconnaissance and exploit vulnerabilities in controlled environments.

I then focused on the defensive side of cybersecurity: learning SOC Analyst L1 responsibilities, investigating security logs, monitoring alerts, correlating events and practising incident response through Splunk and Wazuh home labs.

My goal is to begin my career in a SOC Analyst L1 role and continue developing my skills in threat detection, security monitoring and incident response.
